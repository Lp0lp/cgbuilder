    How to use CGBuilder

    1. Load a molecule — click Load molecule and select a PDB, SDF, GRO, or CIF file. The structure appears in the 3D viewer.
    2. Create beads — click New bead to add a bead and select it (highlighted in blue in the list on the right).
    3. Assign atoms — click an atom in the viewer to add it to the selected bead. Clicking the same atom again increases its weight, shifting the bead centre toward that atom.
    4. Remove atoms — Shift+click an atom to reduce its weight or remove it from the bead.
    5. Edit bead properties — type directly into the Name, Type, and Charge fields on each card.
    6. Predict bead types — once atoms are assigned, click Predict bead types to get Martini 3 type suggestions based on fragment free energies. Click the suggestion chip to apply it.
    7. Inspect the mapping — toggle CG view to overlay bead spheres on the structure. Enable AA surface or CG surface to compare solvent-accessible surface areas and the Δ value.
    8. Export — use the tabs at the bottom of the App page to preview and download your mapping as .py (Python assignments), .gro, .ndx, or .map.
